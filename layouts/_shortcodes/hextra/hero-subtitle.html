Nesting a shortcode does not help: nested output is inlined *before* markdownify and gets stripped too. So {{< line-break >}} emits a token that survives markdownify, and it is swapped for the real

" -}} {{- $content := .Inner | markdownify | replaceRE "\\[\\[fiscus:line-break\\]\\]" $br -}}
